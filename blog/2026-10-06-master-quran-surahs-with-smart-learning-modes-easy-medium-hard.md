## Understanding the Three Learning Modes

QuranSwipe.com provides three different modes to suit various stages of learning:

### Easy Mode: Distinct Word Memorization

In Easy mode, you're tasked with memorizing **distinct words** used in a given Surah. This mode is perfect for beginners or those who want to build a strong vocabulary base.

- **What it does**: Presents all unique words found in the selected surah.
- **How it works**: You’re prompted to recall each word and its meaning.
- **Key feature**: If you get an answer wrong, the correct answer is shown immediately for reinforcement.

> 💡 Tip: Use this mode to create flashcards for repeated review. You can even bookmark unfamiliar terms for later study.

### Medium Mode: Meaning-Based Ayah Comprehension

Medium mode shifts focus from memorizing words to understanding **ayahs as whole units**. You’re given the meaning of an ayah and must identify it from a list of options.

- **What it does**: Helps you grasp the contextual meaning of each verse.
- **How it works**: A sentence or phrase related to the ayah is shown, and you select the correct translation.
- **Key feature**: Encourages deeper comprehension without relying on rote memorization.

> 🧠 Best Practice: Combine this with audio playback to reinforce pronunciation and tone.

### Hard Mode: Fill-in-the-Blank Challenge

Hard mode takes learning a step further by challenging your memory in a **fill-in-the-blank format**. You're shown a partially filled ayah and must complete it correctly.

- **What it does**: Tests both recall and structural understanding of Surahs.
- **How it works**: Words are removed from the ayah, and you have to fill them in correctly.
- **Key feature**: Designed for advanced learners aiming to master recitation and structure.

> ⏱️ Pro Tip: Try the timed version to simulate real-world practice under pressure—great for exam prep or competition training!
