## Understanding the Quran Swipe Gameplay

The *Quran Swipe* game offers multiple themes derived from the Holy Quran, each containing various stages that challenge players' knowledge and decision-making skills. Each theme, such as patience or humility, is composed of a set of words related to its core concept. 

### Key Features of a Theme Stage

- **Word Set:** A curated collection of Arabic words (or phrases) tied to the theme.
- **Objective:** Players must identify and remove words that are NOT related to the theme.
- **Scoring System:** Accuracy in filtering increases player score.

Let’s take a look at how this works with the **Patience Theme**:

```text
Words related to Patience (Sabar):
- صَابِرٍ
- اِسْتَصْبَارًا
- سَبَرَ
- صَابِرَة
- مُصْتَبِرٍ
```

These words are all associated with the concept of patience and perseverance. The player's task is to swipe away any word that does **not** fit this theme.

## How to Play the Patience Stage

1. **Start the Game:** Begin by selecting a theme (e.g., *Patience*).
2. **Identify Words:** Review each word presented on screen.
3. **Swipe Out Irrelevant Words:** Swipe left or right based on whether the word relates to the theme.
4. **Score Points:** Accurate decisions lead to higher scores and better performance.

### Example Gameplay

In one session, a player ended up scoring 13,500 points by successfully identifying and removing most of the distractions:

```text
Distractions (Non-Patience Words):
- فَتُصْبِحَهُ
- يَخْرُجُوا قَوْلًا إِتَاعَ السَّادَة
- بِلْجُنَاء ثَوَاب الْيَتَامَى
- فَانْتَهَلَ الْقِطْرِ بَعْدِهِم
- الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَالْقَيْنَة
- وَلَيْسَ أَرْسِلْهُ نَصِيبًا
```

One mistake occurred when the player hesitated on *فَتُصْبِحَهُ* (which actually relates to patience due to its root *صَبَر*), but mistakenly let it pass, impacting the final score.

## Tips for Effective Gameplay

- **Root Recognition:** Learn common roots of Arabic words, as they often carry thematic meaning.
- **Focus & Speed:** Balance accuracy with quick decision-making.
- **Practice Makes Progress:** Repeated exposure to themes improves pattern recognition.
