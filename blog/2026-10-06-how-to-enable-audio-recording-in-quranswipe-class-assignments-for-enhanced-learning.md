## Introduction

In the ever-evolving landscape of digital education, tools that support interactive and immersive learning experiences are more valuable than ever. For educators using **QuranSwipe**, integrating audio recording capabilities into class assignments can significantly enhance how students engage with Quranic Arabic vocabulary and pronunciation.

Whether you're a teacher looking to assess student progress or a developer aiming to improve user experience within the platform, this guide will walk you through enabling and utilizing audio recording features directly from your class settings.

## Setting Up Your Class Assignment with Audio Recording

Before diving into the setup process, make sure you're logged in as a teacher in the QuranSwipe dashboard. Here's what you'll need to do step-by-step:

### Step 1: Create a New Task or Assessment

1. Navigate to your class settings.
2. Click on **Create Assignment** or **New Task**.
3. Choose a **Custom Deck** (in this example, we’ll use a small deck with three words).

```text
Example Deck:
- Bismillah (In the name of)
- Alhamdulillah (All praise is due to Allah)
- Allah (God)
```

### Step 2: Enable Student Audio Recording

Once you've selected your deck, locate the **"Enable Student Audio Recording"** checkbox. This feature allows students to record their own pronunciation and meaning of each word.

> 🔧 *Tip:* If you're reusing an existing assignment, this setting may already be populated with previous data.

### Step 3: Set Due Date and Requirements

1. Select a due date for the homework (e.g., Thursday).
2. Toggle the **mandatory** or **optional** audio recording option:
   - For mandatory recordings, students must submit an audio file per word.
   - Optional allows flexibility but encourages deeper engagement.

### Step 4: Finalize and Launch Assignment

After configuring all options, click **Create Assignment**. The assignment will now appear in the student dashboard with clear instructions and a timer (typically five seconds per recording).

## Student Experience During the Assignment

Once students access the assignment:

1. They’ll see each word from your deck.
2. Instead of selecting from multiple-choice options, they’re prompted to record their audio response.
3. The system provides a 5-second window per word to complete the recording.

> 🎙️ *Best Practice:* Encourage students to record both pronunciation and meaning for better comprehension.

## Reviewing Student Submissions

As a teacher, once students submit their recordings:

1. Go to your **Grade Book** section.
2. View completed assignments (e.g., Ellie scored 100% on the assessment).
3. Click into any submission to play back the audio file directly from the platform.

> 📊 *Insight:* Teachers can easily evaluate both accuracy of pronunciation and understanding of meaning.

## Conclusion

Integrating audio recording features into QuranSwipe class assignments is not just a technological upgrade—it's a powerful way to personalize learning, support memorization, and encourage active participation among students. Whether you're creating quick assessments or structured homework, this feature ensures that each student’s effort is recognized and evaluated in a meaningful way.

So go ahead, enable audio recording today, and watch your students' engagement and mastery grow!
