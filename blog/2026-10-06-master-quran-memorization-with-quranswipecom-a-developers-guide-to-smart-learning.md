## Understanding the Word Map Feature

Upon visiting [QuranSwipe.com](https://www.quranswipe.com), switch to **Word Map** mode to explore how the platform organizes all distinct words from the Quran. There are 25 stages in total, ranging from beginner-friendly levels like *Alif Baby Step* and *Noor*, to advanced stages such as *Tajweed*, *Trainee*, and finally, *Quran Grandmaster*. 

Each stage is designed based on word frequency—meaning the most commonly used words appear at earlier stages, while less frequent or more complex terms are reserved for later levels.

```bash
# Example: Navigating Stages
1. Start at Alif Baby Step
2. Progress through stages using the center button or direct navigation
3. Reach Quran Grandmaster to tackle rare and challenging words
```

## Exploring Different Learning Modes

QuranSwipe.com supports three learning modes that increase in difficulty:

### Easy Mode
- Teaches distinct words used in the Quran.
- Ideal for beginners starting their journey.

### Medium Mode
- Focuses on understanding ayah meanings.
- Helps deepen comprehension alongside memorization.

### Hard Mode
- Challenges users to fill in blanks or reconstruct ayahs.
- Best suited for advanced learners aiming for mastery.

You can toggle between these modes using the UI controls directly on the website.

## Tracking Progress and Learning from Mistakes

QuranSwipe keeps track of your mistakes and allows you to revisit them for targeted practice. After completing a stage, you can view your report card, print out results, or share your progress with others.

```bash
# Example: Revisiting Mistakes
1. Click on the last stage again
2. Select "Review My Mistakes"
3. Practice only the words you got wrong
```

This feature ensures that your learning is not just passive, but strategic and adaptive—helping you strengthen weak areas.
