## Introduction

Are you a developer or educator looking to streamline language learning and feedback processes? If so, you're in for a treat. In this tutorial, we’ll walk you through the **Comantech Audio Recording Tool**, a powerful utility designed to let students record audio directly from their browser and share it with teachers or mentors. Whether you're working on a course module, practicing pronunciation, or submitting assignments, this tool makes the process seamless.

## Getting Started with the Audio Recording Tool

Before diving in, make sure you have access to the Comantech platform and are logged into your account. Here’s how to locate and launch the tool:

1. Click on the **hamburger menu** (☰) in the top-left corner of the screen.
2. Navigate to the **Tools** section.
3. Look for the tool named **"Record and Share Audio"**.
4. Click **Launch Tool**.

Alternatively, if you're already inside a course:

1. Go to your desired course page.
2. Locate the **Record and Share Audio** button (usually prominently displayed).
3. Click to start recording—no need to navigate away from the course!

## Recording Your Audio

Once launched, the tool will prompt you to allow microphone access. Grant permission when prompted to begin recording.

- **Minimize Support**: The recording continues in the background even if you minimize the tool.
- **Real-time Feedback Loop**: As a student, you can record your voice while reading or practicing lessons—perfect for language learners.

## Best Practices for Effective Use

To get the most out of this tool:

- **Test your microphone** before starting a session.
- **Keep recordings concise**—ideal for short practice or feedback sessions.
- **Use consistent naming conventions** when saving and sharing files.
- **Leverage the browser-based interface** to avoid installing extra software.
